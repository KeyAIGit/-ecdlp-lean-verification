# Research Engine typed-evidence iteration

Date: 2026-07-25  
Scope: architecture and desk decisions only  
Experiment authorization: none

## Decision

Hypothesis generation begins from a materialized mechanism/property cell, not
from a free-form model prompt or a route/tag cross product. The canonical layer
is `repo/ECDLP_TYPED_EVIDENCE_V0.json`; its generated state is
`data/typed_evidence_state.json`.

Each cell binds:

- a mechanism and exact scope;
- the target properties it requires;
- claim-level evidence and local extraction or verifier paths;
- the cost quantity the mechanism would have to change;
- barriers and reopening conditions;
- a deterministic status and evidence digest.

A violated target property produces a scoped desk decision. An unresolved cell
may emit a non-executable research question. Neither path authorizes a run.

## Scientific corrections retained

1. Direct FHJRV rational two-torsion symmetrization is inapplicable to
   secp256k1 over its base field because the target has no nonidentity rational
   two-torsion. This says nothing about auxiliary curves, extension fields, or a
   different sum-compatible action.
2. The independent-cube GLV quotient is closed only in its exact fixed-target
   coordinate-scaling scope. Faithful phase-preserving, non-scalar, and
   birational mechanisms remain outside that result.
3. The PKC 2016 arity-four smooth-subgroup instance fails the target's
   factor-base-size requirement. This does not close the construction at every
   arity.
4. The source's relation-yield balance is stricter than the raw
   `p^(1/m)` size condition. The small-factor product `564522` first clears both
   exact integer thresholds at arity sixteen: `65536` for `p^(1/16)` and
   `445676` for `(16! p)^(1/16)`. This arithmetic screen leaves the
   corresponding S17 construction, generalized-root representation, recovery,
   and total cost unpriced, so no solver run is justified.
5. The PKC 2016 auxiliary curve is not required to be isogenous to secp256k1.
   It is an auxiliary curve over the same field; its own smooth-kernel isogeny
   defines the composed root map. The next obligation is an explicit instance
   or scoped no-instance result plus complete precomputation pricing.

## Intake boundary

The six original Research Engine candidates are retained as a code-anchored,
hash-frozen non-executable regression fixture. Their scientific flags cannot be
edited into admissibility. A future executable candidate must be compiled from
both:

1. one unresolved typed cell; and
2. one digest-bound proposal that clears the five-role adversarial review.

That compiler is intentionally not implemented in this iteration. Quality
clearance therefore remains non-executable.

## Current question classes

- `proposal_required`: creative work is allowed only if it supplies a genuinely
  new premise, exact mechanism, recovery map, and audited cost bridge.
- `desk_cost_bridge_required`: derive a symbolic size and complete cost bound
  before requesting solver compute.
- `property_resolution_required`: construct or refute the missing target
  property with provenance and arithmetic, without an ECDLP run.

The correct success condition for this iteration is a smaller, better-typed
uncertainty set. It is not a positive attack result.
