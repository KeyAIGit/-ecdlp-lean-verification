# UORC-056 H-RPCX rational semiconjugacy barrier V7

## Status

Scoped paper theorem with exact secp256k1 arithmetic instantiation and deterministic replay.

It is not yet formalized end to end in Lean. It does not rule out huge-degree maps computed by small DAG circuits, multicoordinate nonlinear states, or query-dependent random-access evaluators.

## Model

Let `E` be the elliptic curve and let translation by the public generator be

`tau(P)=P+G`.

The subgroup generated by `G` has odd prime order `n`.

Consider a one-coordinate projective state

`S:E->P^1`

of morphism degree `delta`, together with one fixed rational update

`F:P^1->P^1`

of degree `D`.

Assume that on every subgroup point

`S(P+G)=F(S(P))`.

The parity decoder applied to the state may be any deterministic function. The theorem concerns only the autonomous state update.

## Finite-to-global lemma

Compare the two maps

`S after tau`

and

`F after S`.

Two distinct maps from `E` to `P^1` can agree only at a bounded number of points.

The equality locus is the pullback of the diagonal in `P^1 x P^1`. Its degree is at most

`degree(S after tau)+degree(F after S)`

which equals

`delta+D*delta=(D+1)*delta`.

Therefore, if the two maps agree on all `n` subgroup points and

`n>(D+1)*delta`,

they cannot be distinct. The recurrence must then hold as a global rational identity on the whole curve.

## Global rigidity lemma

Assume now that the identity is global:

`S after tau = F after S`.

Translation by `G` has order `n`, so iterating `n` times gives

`S = F^n after S`.

A nonconstant morphism from a complete curve to `P^1` is surjective over the algebraic closure. Hence

`F^n = identity on P^1`.

Degrees multiply under composition:

`degree(F^n)=D^n`.

The identity map has degree one, so

`D^n=1`.

Therefore

`D=1`.

The fixed update is forced to be a Mobius transformation. A genuinely nonlinear global rational update of degree at least two is impossible.

## Exact tradeoff

Combining the two lemmas gives:

- if `D>=2`, then exact subgroup semiconjugacy requires

  `(D+1)*delta>=n`;

- if `D=1`, the update is Mobius and V6 applies.

Thus a one-coordinate rational autonomous state cannot have both state degree and update degree polynomial in `log n`.

## What V7 closes

V7 closes a broad natural architecture:

1. one rational state coordinate;
2. one position-independent rational update;
3. exact update identity on every subgroup point;
4. state degree and update degree both polynomial in `log n`;
5. any deterministic final decoder.

A clever nonlinear decoder does not rescue this model.

## What remains open

V7 is a degree theorem, not an arithmetic-circuit theorem.

A rational function may have enormous degree and still be evaluated by a short circuit through repeated composition or repeated squaring. Therefore V7 does not exclude:

- a state map of exponential degree but polynomial DAG size;
- a multicoordinate nonlinear state;
- updates depending explicitly on the current public coordinates;
- a direct evaluator that does not walk under `Q->Q+G`;
- modular-composition jump-ahead;
- coherent CM, Miller, theta, elliptic-unit, or p-adic transport.

## Research consequence

The remaining positive search is now narrower.

Another low-degree one-coordinate rational recurrence is not new. A surviving candidate must show one of the following:

- explicit high degree with genuinely low DAG size;
- several interacting coordinates with a nonlinear update not reducible to a one-dimensional factor;
- a query-dependent jump-ahead identity;
- or a nonlocal oriented transport that computes the requested state directly from `E,G,Q`.
