## 1. Decision

Within the homogeneous quadratic-normalization algebraic category `C_quad`
defined below, a section with an odd residual EDS-residue factor **cannot have
an intrinsic GLV normalization multiplier different from the perfectly
periodic point-function multiplier**.

This settles the section-multiplier alternative in the requested category. It
does **not** rule out a separate public weight-zero function that directly
computes the carry `g(Q)`. Multiplying a standard `gR3` section by such a
function would isolate `R3`, but that would be a direct carry decoder rather
than a new line-bundle multiplier. The simplest such functions were screened
below and show no scaling signal; the unrestricted weight-zero problem remains
open.

The result is scoped. It is not a universal impossibility statement about
mixed-weight rational circuits with a new trivialization, global analytic theta
branches, p-adic continuation, or arbitrary algorithms outside `C_quad`.

## 2. Exact scope contract

An admitted homogeneous section `A` has two pieces of binary bookkeeping in
its transformation law:

```text
A([k]G)
  = coefficient_A(Q) * s^q_A(k) * rho_G([k]G)^epsilon_A,

q_A(k)=a_A*k^2+b_A*k+c_A,
epsilon_A in {0,1}.
```

Here:

- `s` is the fixed quadratic character of the chosen EDS normalization;
- `rho_G` is the genuinely nonpublic residual EDS character;
- `coefficient_A(Q)` is a public weight-zero coefficient;
- the theorem classifies the **intrinsic normalization factor** `s^q_A(k)`,
  not every possible value pattern of `coefficient_A(Q)`.

The category invariant is

```text
epsilon_A = a_A+b_A                 (mod 2).       (I)
```

Thus `epsilon_A=1` means that, after the multiplication-law and standard
normalization cancellations, one nonpublic residual EDS factor remains. It is
not a raw count of displayed division-polynomial terms. For example, an
even-index pullback may contain one written EDS value while its exponent of the
base hidden residue is even and therefore public.

`C_quad` is the smallest class generated by the perfectly periodic point
section and homogeneous division/net-polynomial sections and closed under:

1. tensor products and duals;
2. fixed integral-index pullbacks and fixed public translations;
3. GLV, Frobenius, and fixed bounded-isogeny transports whose subgroup action
   is public;
4. finite invariant jets;
5. homogeneous sums sharing one normalization law;
6. fixed-rank elliptic-net constructions;
7. products over the order-three GLV orbit.

Public weight-zero coefficients may multiply a section, but they do not change
its intrinsic pair `(epsilon_A,a_A+b_A)`. If such a coefficient itself decodes
`g(Q)`, that is recorded as the separate direct-carry escape rather than as a
counterexample to multiplier rigidity.

The generators satisfy (I), and the closure operations preserve it:

- tensor/dual add or negate both binary weights;
- under `k -> r+t*k`, the residual exponent is multiplied by `t^2`, while the
  quadratic weight changes by the same parity because `t^2=t mod 2`;
- invariant jets leave the index-dependent normalization scalar unchanged;
- fixed-rank net pullbacks have exact even source coefficients;
- homogeneous sums are admitted only within one common linearization class.

### Outside the theorem

The following are not classified by the no-go:

- a weight-zero coefficient whose public value already equals the carry;
- a mixed-weight sum supplied with a new canonical public trivialization;
- interpolation tables whose size grows with subgroup order;
- arbitrary rational circuits not derived from the stated operations;
- global theta/sigma monodromy with a new branch choice;
- p-adic continuation from the formal group to all prime-to-`p` torsion points.

## 3. C3 parity theorem

Let `k0,k1,k2` be the canonical scalar representatives of one GLV orbit in an
odd-order subgroup:

```text
k0+k1+k2=gamma*n,
gamma in {1,2}.
```

For

```text
q(k)=a*k^2+b*k+c,
```

one has

```text
q(k0)+q(k1)+q(k2)
  = (a+b)*gamma+c                 (mod 2).       (R)
```

Indeed,

```text
ki^2=ki mod 2,
n=1 mod 2,
3*c=c mod 2.
```

Combining (R) with (I):

```text
epsilon_A=1
  -> the intrinsic C3 normalization contains gamma up to a fixed sign;

epsilon_A=0
  -> the intrinsic C3 normalization is carry-free, but no odd residual EDS
     factor remains.
```

Therefore no homogeneous section inside `C_quad` has the intrinsic combination

```text
odd residual EDS weight + different/no GLV carry multiplier.
```

## 4. Why the closure operations do not escape

### Fixed affine pullbacks

The quadratic weight changes by the square of the public slope. Since

```text
t^2=t mod 2,
```

an odd-slope pullback preserves the same carry class, while an even-slope
pullback removes both the carry and the odd residual EDS dependence.

### Fixed-rank joint elliptic nets

Increasing net rank creates no missing binary normalization. In the
integral-matrix net transformation law, the coefficient attached to one source
point with net-index coordinate `v`, where `r` is the sum of the other
coordinates, is

```text
v + (v^2-v*r) + v*r
  = v*(v+1),
```

which is always even. The terms are respectively the output point-label
conversion, the unary source exponent, and all pair-source exponents involving
that point.

The tempting rank-three term `Psi_(1,1,1)` on

```text
(Q,phi Q,phi^2 Q)
```

also degenerates structurally. Its explicit numerator is

```text
y0*(x1-x2)+y1*(x2-x0)+y2*(x0-x1).
```

All three `j=0` GLV-orbit points share the same `y`, so the numerator vanishes.
In the sigma presentation, the first normal derivative along the zero-sum
divisor reduces to a fixed unit after cancellation of the three sigma factors.
It supplies no new binary phase.

### Finite jets

Differentiating a homogeneous unary section does not differentiate its
index-dependent normalization scalar. Finite invariant jets retain the same
quadratic weight. The rank-three zero-sum case is covered separately above.

### GLV linearization

A phase supplied solely by an order-three GLV linearization satisfies

```text
z^3=1.
```

But

```text
z=(z^2)^2.
```

Every cube root of unity is already a square, so a quadratic character cannot
read a different binary GLV eigenphase.

### Fixed bounded isogenies

A fixed isogeny/root cover acts on the chosen prime-order subgroup through a
public transport, possibly followed by a known scalar map. Its intrinsic binary
effect is governed by the affine-pullback theorem. The previously constructed
Eisenstein cubic root was identified with a public inverse 3-isogeny and
supplied no independent multiplier.

### Homogeneous sums

Sections can be added canonically only inside the same linearized line bundle,
where they share one quadratic normalization. A sum of different weights needs
a new trivialization and is outside `C_quad`.

## 5. Consequence for the known odd aggregate

Recall

```text
R3(Q)=rho(Q)rho(phi Q)rho(phi^2 Q),
C3(Q)=g(Q)R3(Q),
g(Q)=(-1)^gamma(Q).
```

`R3` has odd residual EDS weight. Hence every homogeneous section in `C_quad`
whose raw law contains `R3` has intrinsic orbit normalization

```text
constant * g(Q) * R3(Q),
```

rather than `constant * R3(Q)`.

A public weight-zero coefficient `F(Q)` can cancel this only when its own C3
orbit value supplies `g(Q)`. That is exactly the direct carry-decoder problem,
not a new section multiplier.

The theorem unifies the exact collapses already found for the perfectly
periodic point function, the first order-`n` torsion jet, `psi_(n+1)`,
`psi_(n-1)`, fixed-rank net pullbacks, and finite tensor/dual products.

## 6. Weight-zero carry escape screen

The simplest overlooked direct-carry candidate was

```text
chi(y(Q)).
```

It has the required symmetries on the frozen fields:

```text
chi(y(phi Q))=chi(y(Q)),
chi(y(-Q))=-chi(y(Q)).
```

The bounded screen tested

```text
chi(y(Q)),
chi(y(Q)*(x(Q)^3+a)),              a in F_p,
```

and nondegenerate products of two distinct invariant linear factors.

Protocol:

```text
15 frozen j=0 prime-order toy subgroups,
orders 19 through 4021,
200 matched random anti-Kummer/C3-invariant controls per case,
1,072,350 exact quadratic-orbit parity checks.
```

```text

cases strictly above matched 95% null:         0
```

## 8. Exact answer

```text
different intrinsic multiplier inside C_quad:            impossible by scoped theorem
direct weight-zero carry decoder:                        open in general
simple weight-zero family:                               no scaling signal
order-three GLV eigenphase escape:                       impossible
fixed affine/tensor/dual/unary-jet escape:                impossible
fixed-rank joint-net escape:                             impossible
mixed-weight/global analytic category:                   open
public R3 or carry decoder:                              absent
unconditional sub-square-root ECDLP algorithm:           absent
```

Thus the requested multiplier alternative is resolved for the current
homogeneous algebraic category. The surviving constructive question is no
longer “another standard section,” but either a direct weight-zero carry
decoder or a construction outside `C_quad`.
