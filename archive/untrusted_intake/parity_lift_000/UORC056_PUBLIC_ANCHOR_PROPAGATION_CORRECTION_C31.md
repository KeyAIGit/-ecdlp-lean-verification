# UORC-056 C31 correction: source generation means anchor propagation

The oriented-root normalization provides the public anchor

```text
Y_G(x(G))=-y(G).
```

C31 therefore uses `source generation` in the following precise sense:

```text
generate the full or query-specific oriented source action from the one known anchor at G.
```

It does not mean that no branch-sensitive datum is public. The unresolved operation is propagation or integration of the anchor across the marked subgroup.

The rank theorem remains unchanged: a fixed characteristic-zero linear dictionary containing the complete family of marked-generator source vectors has dimension at least `(n-1)/2`. This does not exclude a nonlinear fixed-G propagation circuit beginning from the canonical anchor.

The corrected successor target is

```text
PUBLIC-ANCHOR-FIXED-G-NONLINEAR-PROPAGATION-082.
```

A valid positive construction must explicitly consume the anchor at `G`, propagate it to `Q`, and charge every nonlocal product, quotient-algebra state, continuation path, or control decision.
