## Fixed input

The sparse determinant is

```text
D_(a,b,c)(G,Q)
  = det(aI+bT_G+cT_Q).
```

When `Q=[k]G`, a chosen regular basis identifies it with

```text
D_(a,b,c)(k)
  = Res_z(z^n-1,a+bz+cz^k).
```

C25 classifies the full S3/Mobius symmetry and closes all zero-coefficient strata, `b=c`, `a=c`, all-equal coefficients, and coefficient-permutation-invariant extraction for every prime order greater than eleven.

C26 proves:

```text
projective cubic coefficients (1,omega,omega^2)
  have an exact order-three phase law,
quadratic-character and zero-status extraction collide at opposite parity,
exact-value extraction remains open,
one S3/Mobius reparameterization has worst-case transformed degree
  at least floor((n-3)/6)+2,
```

and records the control dichotomy:

```text
numeric-k sparse algorithms may use k,
public-Q regular realization has state dimension n.
```

The central unresolved question is whether the determinant or resultant can be evaluated from the public operator pair `(T_G,T_Q)` using sub-square-root total state without recovering `k`.

## Central target


The evaluator must use only public `E,G,Q,n` and a coefficient family with compact public description. It must not receive numeric `k`, a table indexed by scalars, all roots of unity, a degree-`n` polynomial, or an `n`-dimensional matrix.
