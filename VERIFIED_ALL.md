## Why the canonical ledgers are not physically merged

1. **Different meaning.** `VERIFIED.md` is the ECDLP reference-deployment
   ledger. `VERIFIED_RESEARCHOS.md` is the non-ECDLP ResearchOS ledger.
2. **Different schemas.** The ECDLP ledger has five cells per row. ResearchOS
   requires twelve cells, including statement anchors, source contracts,
   review records, dates, axiom bases, and claim scopes.
3. **Different counters.** ECDLP's approximate distinct-result count has a
   curated alternate-form adjustment. That denominator does not transfer to
   ResearchOS.
4. **Different audit paths.** Each ledger has its own generated registry and
   kernel-facing axiom audit. `scripts/check_ledger_isolation.py` prevents the
   ResearchOS rows from silently changing ECDLP headline numbers.

The optimization is therefore an **index over isolated sources**, not a schema
collapse. Add theorem rows only to the appropriate canonical ledger, then run
the normal generators.
